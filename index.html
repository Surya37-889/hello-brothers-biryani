About Us

A Yanam Kitchen Built On Andhra Flavour

Hello Brothers Biryani is a biryani kitchen in Yanam, 533464, where Andhra cooking traditions meet a love for perfectly layered rice. We cook in small batches with whole spices, fresh aromatics and careful dum-style layering — so every plate arrives fragrant and full of character.

The House Speciality

Signature Gongura Biryani

The dish that made our name — a true taste of Andhra, served hot and generous.

Tangy, bold and unmistakably Andhra.

At the heart of our kitchen is gongura — the prized sorrel leaf that gives Andhra cooking its signature sour tang. We fold it into a slow-cooked masala with roasted spices, then layer it through long-grain rice so every spoonful carries that bright, mouth-watering kick.

The result is a biryani that balances heat, tang and aroma — rich and warming, yet fresh and zesty at the same time. It is the flavour regulars come back for, and the one we are proudest to serve.

  • ✦ Tangy gongura (sorrel leaf) masala
  • ✦ Slow-cooked Andhra spice blend
  • ✦ Aromatic long-grain rice, layered dum-style
